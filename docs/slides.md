# Motivation

* Electronic Thesis and Dissertations (ETDs) are a considerable institutional investiment,
* They contain high quality research data, and yet they remain underutlized due to challenges in discovery and access.
* In this work we will explore semantic search tool to unlock the untaped knownledge.

# What is Semantic Search

* Search by the meaning instead of keyword matching.
